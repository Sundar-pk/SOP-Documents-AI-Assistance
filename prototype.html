What is the standard settlement period for equity trades and what happens if settlement fails?
You · 10:42 AM
🧠
Based on the Settlements SOP v4.2, equity trades follow a T+2 settlement cycle — the transaction must settle within 2 business days of trade date.

In case of settlement failure:
  1. Operations must raise a Fail Notice by 3:30 PM on T+2 via the internal settlements portal.
  2. The counterparty is notified within 30 minutes of the fail notice.
  3. If unresolved by T+4, the trade is escalated to the Middle Office Head for buy-in proceedings.
  4. All failed trades must be logged in the Daily Fail Register with reason codes.

Who is responsible for approving the buy-in decision?
You · 10:44 AM
🧠
The buy-in decision follows a dual-approval process:

• Primary approval: Middle Office Head (T+4 onwards)
• Secondary approval: Risk Manager must co-sign for trades exceeding ₹50 Lakhs notional value
• For cross-border settlements, the Head of Settlements must be notified additionally as per SEBI guidelines.
